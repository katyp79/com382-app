Rules

⏱ "Max time per answer" caps each response. Students see a gentle note up front ("you have up to X minutes — take your time"); a live countdown only appears near the end, and when it reaches zero the answer auto-submits and the conversation moves on. (Think time is separate, before recording starts.)

🔇 "Must start answering within" stops a student from sitting silently. If they haven't begun speaking within this window after a question, it auto-advances and records "(no answer — didn't begin speaking)". Counts from when recording starts (after think time).

⚠️ If you paste a detailed "discussion source" / answer-key style document, keep this No — otherwise students could read the answers. Students should have gone through the actual module material beforehand.
